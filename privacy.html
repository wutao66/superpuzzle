简要说明：Super Puzzle 是完全离线的本地 App。我们不要求账号，不收集个人信息，也不会上传你选择的照片、视频或拼图记录。In short: Super Puzzle is a fully offline, local App. We require no account, collect no personal information, and never upload your selected photos, videos, or puzzle activity.

1. 概述

Super Puzzle 是一款无需账号、无需联网的本地拼图应用。本 App 不主动建立网络连接，不运营服务器，也不会向开发者或第三方上传您的数据。

2. 不收集的信息

本 App 不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、账号、位置、通讯录、设备标识、使用分析、广告标识或行为追踪信息，不包含广告或第三方分析服务。

3. 照片与视频

只有在您主动使用系统照片选择器时，本 App 才能读取您明确选择的照片或视频。素材仅在设备本地用于预览、切片和拼图。

  • 照片在使用期间保存在应用内存中。
  • 所选视频会复制到 App 的临时目录供本地播放，并在更换素材或离开相关体验后删除。
  • 照片、视频和拼图记录不会被上传。

4. 本地存储

您选择的界面语言会保存在设备的 App 偏好设置中。拼图进度和所选媒体不会同步到服务器，本 App 也不提供云端备份。卸载 App 时，系统会移除其本地数据。

5. 系统服务

本 App 通过 Apple 提供的系统照片选择器获取单次选择的媒体，不要求访问完整照片图库。Apple 和 App Store 可能依照其自身政策处理诊断、购买或分发信息，该等处理不由本 App 控制。

6. 儿童隐私

由于本 App 不收集或传输个人信息，因此也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。监护人仍应根据实际情况指导儿童使用设备和媒体内容。

7. 协议更新

如功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本协议将随 App 更新，并在本页面说明新的生效日期。

1. Overview

Super Puzzle is a local puzzle App that requires no account and no network connection. The App does not initiate network connections, operate a server, or upload your data to the developer or third parties.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, accounts, locations, contacts, device identifiers, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics services.

3. Photos and Videos

The App reads a photo or video only after you explicitly select it through Apple's system photo picker. Media is processed locally for preview, slicing, and puzzle play.

  • Photos remain in app memory while in use.
  • A selected video is copied to the App's temporary directory for local playback and deleted when you replace the media or leave the related experience.
  • Photos, videos, and puzzle activity are never uploaded.

4. Local Storage

Your language choice is stored in the App's local preferences. Puzzle progress and selected media are not synchronized to a server, and the App provides no cloud backup. The system removes local App data when the App is uninstalled.

5. System Services

The App uses Apple's system photo picker to receive only the media you select and does not request full photo-library access. Apple and the App Store may process diagnostic, purchase, or distribution information under their own policies; that processing is outside this App's control.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, it does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Guardians should still supervise device and media use as appropriate.

7. Policy Updates

If the App's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated with the App and will show a new effective date on this page.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, email wt1377126991@163.com or visit the Support Center. Do not include private photos or videos in a public report.
