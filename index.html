常见问题COMMON QUESTIONS

开始之前Before you begin

App 需要联网吗？Does the App need internet access?

不需要。核心功能完全离线运行，也没有账号、广告或分析服务。No. Core features run entirely offline, with no accounts, ads, or analytics.

会访问整个照片图库吗？Does it access my whole photo library?

不会。App 使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，只能读取你明确选择的素材。No. The App uses Apple's system picker and receives only the media you explicitly choose.

支持哪些设备？Which devices are supported?

支持运行 iOS 或 iPadOS 16 及以上版本的 iPhone 和 iPad。iPhone and iPad devices running iOS or iPadOS 16 and later are supported.
